Privacy Policy

Ads Invoices · Last updated October 12, 2026

What this app is

Ads Invoices is a personal script that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and it has no server, website backend or database.

Data it accesses

  • Google Ads invoices: invoice details, such as invoice ID, dates and amounts, and invoice PDFs for the owner's own Google Ads account.
  • Gmail send permission: used only to send the invoice email from the owner's account to addresses the owner configures. The app cannot read, search, change or delete email.

How the data is used and stored

  • Invoices are emailed to the recipients the owner chooses, and a copy of each PDF is saved on the owner's computer.
  • The sign-in token and a list of already-sent invoice IDs are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party, apart from Google's own APIs, which are used to fetch the invoices and send the email.
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Ads Invoices' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the script's folder removes all locally stored data.
