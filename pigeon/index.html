Pigeon

Pigeon is an independent Gmail app for iPhone. Add one or more Gmail accounts to read, search, write and organize your mail, with push notifications, snooze, scheduled send and offline changes.

Pigeon talks to Gmail directly from your phone. Its optional notification relay handles only what Gmail can't do on a phone: push notifications, scheduled send and snooze.

It asks Google for these permissions:

  • Read, compose, send and organize Gmail (gmail.modify): to show your mail, send messages and drafts, and archive, label, star or trash conversations when you ask it to. It cannot permanently delete mail.
  • Your email address (openid, email): to tell your accounts apart in the app.

Pigeon is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Gmail is a trademark of Google LLC.
