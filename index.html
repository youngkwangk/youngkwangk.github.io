Ads Invoices

Ads Invoices is a small personal script that runs on its owner's own computer. Once a month it downloads the Google Ads invoice PDF for the owner's own Google Ads account and emails it as an attachment to the owner's chosen billing address.

It is not a public service. Only its owner signs in to it, and it accesses only their own Google Ads invoices and Gmail sending.

It uses two Google permissions:

  • Google Ads (adwords): to list the account's monthly invoices and download their PDFs.
  • Gmail, send only (gmail.send): to email each invoice from the owner's account. It cannot read, search or delete mail.
